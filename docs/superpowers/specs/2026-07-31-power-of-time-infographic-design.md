# Power of Time Infographic Design

## Purpose

Create a standalone Public Mutual infographic image for use within the Corporate Website. It must explain, at a glance, why beginning an investment plan earlier can give compounding more time to work. It is educational content for an unknown public audience, not a calculator, investment projection, or personal recommendation.

## Core story

Two investors each contribute RM20,000 per year. Jack begins at age 25 and contributes for 10 years; Jill begins at age 35 and contributes for 30 years. Despite Jack contributing less total capital, the illustrative example shows that earlier participation can produce a higher value at age 65 because of the additional time invested.

The message is: **Start earlier. Let time work harder.**

## Content hierarchy

1. Headline: “Start Earlier. Let Time Work Harder.”
2. Supporting statement: “Two investors contribute RM200,000. The earlier start gives compounding more time to grow.”
3. A shared horizontal age timeline from 25 to 65.
4. Jack’s contribution period: age 25–34; RM20,000 annually for 10 years; illustrative value at age 65: RM2,250,731.
5. Jill’s contribution period: age 35–64; RM20,000 annually for 30 years; illustrative value at age 65: RM2,021,461.
6. Closing insight: “Time—not just contribution amount—can make a meaningful difference.”
7. Footer disclosure: “Illustration only. Values and returns are assumptions, not guaranteed.”

## Visual direction

Produce three equally complete image variations for selection. All use a white or soft-neutral background, Public Mutual navy as the structural colour, red to identify Jack, calm blue to identify Jill, and gold reserved for final outcomes. Use friendly editorial-style illustrated people, a single comprehensible chart/timeline, restrained decoration, and ample whitespace. Avoid dense copy, generic stock photography, excessive gradients, and slide-like bullet lists.

### Variation A — Timeline comparison

The recommended lead concept. Two labelled character cards feed into a shared timeline and rising compounding curves. Final-value cards at the right establish the contrast.

### Variation B — Stair-step to growth

Use a clearer, more editorial stepped composition: contribution years shown as recurring deposits, then two diverging curves. This prioritises narrative over chart precision.

### Variation C — Split-path comparison

Two vertically aligned paths show the years each person contributes; they reunite at a large “age 65” moment. This is the most mobile-friendly and least data-dense option.

## Output and constraints

- Deliver three static high-resolution infographic image concepts suitable for website placement.
- Use English copy exactly as documented unless typography needs a non-material line break.
- The figures remain explicitly illustrative; no personalised performance claims or calls to action are included.
- A later website implementation can select one variation and provide accessible alternative text in HTML.
